📚 Cyber Law & Statutory Penal Provisions Matrix

Section 43 read with Section 66 (IT Act)

Offence: Unauthorized Access, Downloading, or Extracting Computer Data.

Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years and/or fine up to ₹5,00,000.

Section 66C (IT Act)

Offence: Identity Theft & Dishonest Use of Passwords / Electronic Signatures / Biometrics.

Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to ₹1,00,000.

Section 66D (IT Act)

Offence: Cheating by Personation using Computer Resources / Communication Devices.

Penalty: Imprisonment up to 3 years and fine up to ₹1,00,000.

Section 66F (IT Act)

Offence: Cyber Terrorism & Attacks on Critical Information Infrastructure (CII).

Penalty: Imprisonment for Life.

Section 72A (IT Act) & DPDP Act 2023

Offence: Disclosure of Information in Breach of Lawful Contract & Data Fiduciary Default.

Penalty: Up to 3 years imprisonment or penalties up to ₹250 Crores under DPDP Act.

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) 2023

Offence: Section 318(4) (Cheating) & Section 319 (Personation) & Section 78 (Stalking).

Application: Parallel criminal prosecution alongside Information Technology Act.
